TriageDesk

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Who operates TriageDesk

TriageDesk is operated by Dan Faenza. It helps authorized race teams coordinate medical-area locations, capacity, and timing. This policy covers triagedeskapp.com and the TriageDesk service. TriageDesk is a day-of-event coordination tool, not a medical-record or records-retention system.

Privacy contact: dan.faenza@gmail.com.

Information processed

  • Account and access information: email addresses, account identifiers, race memberships, roles, and invitations.
  • Google sign-in information: basic identity information supplied through Google and our authentication provider, which may include an account identifier, email address, name, and profile image.
  • Race operational information: race or bib numbers, triage, chief complaints, Notes, source and ETA, bed and pool assignments, movements, dispositions, and timestamps. This information may identify participants and reveal health information, even without a name.
  • Technical and administrative information: authentication, security, and access-management records, and information processed by service providers such as IP addresses and browser details.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, control race access, deliver account and invitation messages, synchronize the board, calculate live counts and timers, and investigate errors or misuse.

Google sign-in requests email and basic profile permissions only. It does not request permission to read Gmail messages, contacts, calendars, or Drive files. Google account information is used for authentication and account identification, not advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Access and service providers

Race membership and role control access. Owners manage teams and configuration; Editors manage patient operations. Viewers see the read-only status board, including assigned race numbers, complaints, triage, timers, and counts, but not Notes or discharged-patient details through that view.

Race organizers control placement of shared displays and must limit access appropriately. A race number is not necessarily anonymous.

We use Supabase for database and authentication, Cloudflare for website delivery, Resend for transactional email, and Google for optional Google sign-in. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. Account and invitation emails contain recipient and account-access information, not patient complaints or board records. Provider processing, logging, and backup practices apply; this policy does not promise storage in a particular country.

Browser storage and security

The app stores authentication session information in browser storage to keep users signed in. Providers may also use security-related storage or cookies. Sign out on shared devices and keep sign-in and invitation links confidential.

The service uses HTTPS and role-based access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Race data and End Race

Patient operational data is held during the race. At the end of each day's tent operations, the owner must use End Race and confirm deletion. This removes patient details, visits, assignments, movements, dispositions, and patient timing histories from the active database and resets beds and pools for reuse. It does not create a report or retain new performance statistics.

Closing the browser, signing out, or reaching midnight does not delete data. There is no automatic seven-day deletion job. Data remains until the owner completes the clearing action.

Race layouts, pod capabilities, accounts, team memberships, invitations, and administrative access records are not deleted by End Race. Accounts and team/invitation records remain until removed or a deletion request is handled. Administrative records are retained for access management and security; there is no scheduled automatic deletion for these records. A minimal reset receipt contains only a random identifier, race identifier, and reset generation, without patient identifiers, counts, or durations.

Active-database deletion does not immediately erase provider backups or technical logs, previously exported or emailed copies, or information still displayed on another device. Provider retention processes apply. Any reports saved under the earlier reporting feature are not automatically erased by End Race; that feature is now disabled.

Your requests and choices

Contact dan.faenza@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion. We may need to verify identity and coordinate with the responsible race organizer. Do not include medical details or sign-in tokens in the initial email. Applicable legal obligations may affect how a request is handled.

Removing race membership does not delete an authentication account. You can revoke the Google connection in your Google Account settings; this does not itself delete information already held by TriageDesk. Other sign-in methods may remain available.

Policy changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes and announce material changes in the app or by email.
